FOR PROFESSIONAL PARTNERS

One local contact for guest requests.

If you manage guest arrangements for a residence, aircraft, yacht or hospitality business, Pearson Concierge can be contacted about selected Nassau ground and lifestyle services. We review the requirement and return with what may be possible.

Built for requests that need careful coordination.

The company is designed for service enquiries from both guests and people who manage their time. A villa host might need provisioning before check-in; an assistant might need an in-villa wellness appointment; an aviation professional might be asked for onward transportation after a flight.

What we can review

Requests for transport, private yacht experiences, wellness, chef dining and villa provisioning. We do not promise availability, immediate response times or preferred provider status before a written agreement is in place.

How partner referrals work

Send the dates, location, guest specifications and requested service. We assess feasibility, clarify any commission or coordination arrangement where applicable and agree the booking process before serving the guest.

Guest privacy matters

Only share the guest details you are authorized to provide. We avoid publishing guest names, flight manifests or sensitive travel details, and confirmations are handled directly with designated contacts.

What to send us

  • Your organization and role
  • Service and location requested
  • Timing and approximate group size
  • Preferred communication contact

We review requests individually. Pricing and availability must be confirmed before any service is booked.
