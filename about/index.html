ABOUT PEARSON CONCIERGE

A considered approach to your time.

Pearson Concierge is a founder-led concierge service being built around a straightforward idea: make it easier for people visiting or staying in The Bahamas to arrange the services they need without coordinating every supplier themselves.

Request an arrangement ↗Browse concierge services ↗

Founded by Amar Pearson.

Amar Pearson is a Bahamian business founder involved in tourism, wellness and digital services. Pearson Concierge brings that practical operator perspective to a simple enquiry-first experience. Rather than promising every service instantly, we start with the requirement and confirm what can be delivered.

What you can expect

Clear communication, proposals before confirmation and a focus on protecting your time and information. Services offered through independent providers are governed by their own applicable terms and availability.

Independent providers, clearly identified

We are not an airline, yacht fleet, hotel, transport fleet or chef agency. Some requests may be fulfilled by founder-associated businesses, including Tranquilitas Spa for suitable wellness services; others require independent specialists.

Where we start

Our initial focus is Nassau and Paradise Island, with other Bahamian destinations considered upon request. No testimonials, partnerships or service availability are implied before they are verified.

Who we can help

  • Private visitors and villa guests
  • Assistants arranging guest services
  • Property hosts and hospitality teams
  • Clients seeking a clear quote before booking

We review requests individually. Pricing and availability must be confirmed before any service is booked.
