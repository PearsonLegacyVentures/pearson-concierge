In-villa massage, on your terms.

Enjoy the convenience of a massage in your villa, hotel accommodation or other suitable private setting. Tell us how many people are receiving treatments, your preferred time and where you are staying. We will review available wellness arrangements before confirming anything.

Bring the experience to your space.

For visitors spending their day by the pool, families staying in a villa or guests planning a quiet couples treatment, mobile massage avoids an extra journey across Nassau. Appointments are coordinated with qualified service providers subject to location, access and scheduling.

Individual, couples and group requests

Ask about Swedish or deep-tissue massage, couples appointments and arrangements for multiple guests. The therapists, service length, equipment and final price will be confirmed based on the specific request.

The Tranquilitas connection

The Pearson family also operates Tranquilitas Spa, an established spa and mobile wellness business in The Bahamas. Where suitable, wellness requests may be fulfilled through Tranquilitas or another appropriate provider. Pearson Concierge is the coordination point, not a claim that every provider is directly employed by us.

What to prepare

Share the property name or area, requested treatments, number of people, preferred date and time, and any access instructions. Confirm the space can safely accommodate massage tables where required.

What to include in your request

  • Villa, hotel or yacht location
  • Number of guests and treatment preference
  • Preferred appointment date and time
  • Any access, mobility or setup requirements

We review requests individually. Pricing and availability must be confirmed before any service is booked.

Frequently asked questions.

Couples treatments can be requested. We will confirm therapist availability, setup and the final price before accepting the booking.

Can the massage take place on a yacht?

Some yacht requests may be possible, subject to safe working space, docking arrangements and provider approval.

Is this connected to Tranquilitas Spa?

Pearson Concierge and Tranquilitas share founder involvement. Tranquilitas may fulfill appropriate wellness requests, subject to availability.
