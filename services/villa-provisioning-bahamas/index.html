Spend less time getting settled.

Villa preparation is not the same as property management. Our initial focus is practical, one-off arrival needs: basic stocking, requested essentials and coordination with the property contact. Ongoing home monitoring and maintenance require separate scoping.

Grocery and household essentials

Requests may include food, water, non-alcoholic beverages, toiletries and household supplies. Availability, substitutions, shopping costs, delivery conditions and any restricted items must be confirmed locally before purchasing.

Arrival and special-occasion details

Let us know about welcome amenities, flowers, celebratory setups or a family’s specific needs. Items are sourced only after approval of the plan and budget.

Villa access and privacy

A property owner, host or authorized manager must approve access. We request only the information needed for fulfillment, and specific handover arrangements are confirmed case by case.

What to include in your request

  • Villa area, arrival date and authorized contact
  • Grocery or household shopping list
  • Budget and substitution preferences
  • Delivery access and any special requests

We review requests individually. Pricing and availability must be confirmed before any service is booked.

BEFORE YOU ARRANGE

Frequently asked questions.

Can groceries be ready before we arrive?

That can be requested. We will confirm shopping, delivery and property access arrangements before promising a completion time.

Do you provide full villa property management?

Not as a standard listed service. Ongoing property management enquiries can be reviewed separately.

Can my property manager make the request?

Yes, an authorized property manager or host can contact us on your behalf with the details needed to coordinate the service.
