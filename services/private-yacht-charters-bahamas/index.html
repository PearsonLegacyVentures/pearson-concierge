PRIVATE CHARTERS / NASSAU

Private yacht charters, thoughtfully arranged.

Plan a private day on the water from Nassau or Paradise Island without handling every enquiry yourself. Tell us the date, group size and kind of experience you have in mind. We can review available options with independent charter operators and return with details before you decide.

The day is yours. The details matter.

A good charter starts with the right boat for your group, not a generic list of tours. We begin with your timing, number of guests, departure preference and plans for the day. A relaxed cruise, a swim-and-snorkel itinerary or a celebration may require different vessels and amenities.

What can be included

Depending on the operator and vessel, requests can cover private half-day or full-day trips, captain and crew, swimming stops, food and beverage preferences, or support for special occasions. Every inclusion must be confirmed in the operator’s written proposal.

Where do trips start?

Many private charters depart from Nassau or Paradise Island marinas. Routes to Rose Island and nearby cays may be considered, but destinations depend on vessel capabilities, the weather, sea conditions and operator permissions. We do not promise an itinerary before it is approved.

Before confirming a charter

We recommend checking capacity, cancellation terms, safety equipment, weather policy, transfer requirements, taxes, fuel costs and any gratuities or extras. Pearson Concierge coordinates enquiries; charters are operated by independent providers, not by Pearson Concierge.

What to include in your request

  • Preferred date and departure time
  • Number of adults and children
  • Departure location and duration
  • Any food, accessibility or celebration needs

We review requests individually. Pricing and availability must be confirmed before any service is booked.

BEFORE YOU ARRANGE

Frequently asked questions.

Can I request a private yacht from Nassau?

Yes. Send your date, party size and preferred experience. We will check whether an independent operator can accommodate it.

Can we visit Rose Island?

Rose Island may be an option on suitable private charters. The final itinerary depends on the vessel, operator and conditions on the day.

Do you own or operate the yachts?

No. We help coordinate charter requests with independent operators, subject to their availability, licenses and terms.
