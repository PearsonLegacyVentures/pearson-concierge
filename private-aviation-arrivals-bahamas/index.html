PRIVATE AVIATION / GROUND SERVICES

When the flight lands, the details are covered.

Flying privately into Nassau involves more than the flight. Personal assistants, flight departments and guests can contact Pearson Concierge about ground transfers, villa arrangements, dining and wellness after arrival.

A ground-side concierge contact in Nassau.

Our role is practical coordination beyond the aircraft. Share the arrival schedule, terminal or handler instructions where permitted, passenger requirements and local destination. We can help review arrangements with relevant service providers before the travel day.

Arrivals and onward transfers

Independent transportation partners may be able to provide private transfers from an approved meeting point to a villa, hotel or marina. Aviation facility access and passenger handling remain under the relevant operator and FBO rules.

Arrival-ready villas and personal services

Pair an arrival with grocery requests, in-villa massage, a private chef enquiry or a water experience during the stay. Arrangements are confirmed individually to avoid overpromising a bundled package.

Aviation boundaries

Pearson Concierge is not an aircraft operator, charter broker or flight-handling company. We do not provide aircraft, pilots, flight plans, customs clearance or ramp privileges. Aviation operations must be managed by appropriately authorized parties.

What to include in your request

  • Arrival date, time and terminal or approved meeting point
  • Party size, luggage and onward address
  • Contact for the passenger or authorized assistant
  • Any additional villa, dining or wellness requests

We review requests individually. Pricing and availability must be confirmed before any service is booked.

BEFORE YOU ARRANGE

Frequently asked questions.

Can Pearson Concierge book my private jet?

No. We focus on ground-side personal services after arrival and can review introductions separately when appropriate.

Can you meet guests at the aircraft?

No ramp-side meet-and-greet access is promised. Pickup points are determined by the airport, handling provider and authorized transport supplier.

Can my flight department send the enquiry?

Yes. An authorized assistant, flight department or host may submit relevant ground-service requirements for coordination.
