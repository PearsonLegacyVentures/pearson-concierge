Nassau & Paradise Island, arranged around you.

Staying in a private residence, arriving at Lynden Pindling International Airport or visiting Paradise Island? Pearson Concierge helps coordinate services around the practical details of a Nassau stay, with one enquiry and a confirmed plan.

Local arrangements, one clear point of contact.

Nassau and Paradise Island have a wide range of accommodations, marinas, dining and experiences. Coordinating different providers can create needless friction, particularly when arrival times or group needs change. We focus on specific requests, with provider availability and pricing made clear before confirmation.

From the airport to your accommodation

Send an airport or private aviation arrival request with your terminal, flight details and destination. Private drivers and transfers are arranged through suitable independent suppliers, subject to their availability and access requirements.

From villas to days on the water

Request private chef dining, massage at your villa, provisioning or a charter from a local marina. These are separate specialist services, so each is confirmed on its own terms.

Other islands in The Bahamas

Our listed focus is Nassau and Paradise Island, on New Providence. We can review requests for other Bahamian islands, but we do not claim a confirmed supplier network nationwide.

What to include in your request

  • Nassau or Paradise Island location
  • Travel dates and group size
  • Services to arrange
  • A local host or property contact if relevant

We review requests individually. Pricing and availability must be confirmed before any service is booked.
